Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website. This page says what we have done, what we have not yet checked, and how to tell us about a problem.

Last updated 15 November 2026

Our aim

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. We have not had it independently audited, so we do not claim full conformance.

What we have done

Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible outline showing where you are and a “Skip to main content” link at the top. Body text is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a typeface designed for legibility, and the pages adjust to phone, tablet and computer screens. Animation is reduced or switched off if your device is set to reduce motion. Each infographic comes with a plain-English summary.

How we have checked

The website has been developed with accessibility in mind, including keyboard navigation, readable typography, responsive layouts and reduced-motion preferences. A comprehensive accessibility audit, including testing with assistive technologies, has not yet been completed. We will continue to review accessibility as the website develops.

What we have not yet checked

We have not yet completed testing with screen readers, across all major browsers or on a wide range of devices. Some interactive charts and image-based infographics may be difficult to use with assistive technologies. Although we provide accompanying explanations and summaries, these may not communicate every detail contained in the visual materials. If you need information in an alternative accessible format, please contact us by email.

Tell us about a problem

If you cannot use part of this website, or you need information in a different format, email a.vostanis@kent.ac.uk. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do and what got in the way. We will reply as soon as we can and fix what we can.

If you are not satisfied

If you contact us about an accessibility problem and are not happy with our reply, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service at equalityadvisoryservice.com.
