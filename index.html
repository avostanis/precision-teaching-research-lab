01About the lab

Research that measures learning as it happens, and puts the data in the hands of teachers and learners.

Who we are

The Precision Teaching Research Lab is a research group led by Dr Thanos Vostanis, based at the Tizard Centre, University of Kent, part of LASE University Group. It brings together doctoral researchers and collaborators who share an interest in precision teaching and evidence-based approaches to teaching and learning for people of all ages.

Who we work with

Most of our work is with people with intellectual and developmental disabilities and with special educational needs and disabilities. Our work is also collaborative and interdisciplinary: we work with practitioners, services and researchers from other fields, in education and well beyond it.

How we work

We choose methods to fit each project, whether quantitative or qualitative, single-case or group. Above all, we want the work to be useful: it should make sense in the place it is meant for, matter to the people involved, and make a real difference to their lives.

Open data and materials

We share what sits behind our studies wherever we can: the Standard Celeration Charts, datasheets, fidelity checklists and questionnaires. They are openly available on Figshare, so that others can check our work and build on it.
