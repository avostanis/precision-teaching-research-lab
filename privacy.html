Privacy notice

This website is built to collect as little as possible about the people who visit it. This page explains what that means in practice.

Last updated 14 November 2026 · Draft copy · to confirm

Who runs this website

The PT Research Lab website is run by Dr Thanos Vostanis, founder of the PT Research Lab, Tizard Centre, University of Kent. For anything in this notice, email a.vostanis@kent.ac.uk.

What the website collects

Nothing about you is collected by the pages themselves. There is no contact form, no account or login, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising or social-media tracking. The fonts and the code that make the pages work are served from this website, not from other companies, so your visit is not passed to them.

What the hosting company may record

Like any website, this one is delivered by a hosting company, which may keep basic technical records of each visit, such as the internet address of the device, the time and the page requested. They are kept by the host for running and securing the service. We do not see or use them for the lab. The host’s own privacy statement explains how it handles them.

If you email us

If you write to a.vostanis@kent.ac.uk, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply and to deal with your enquiry, and we keep it only as long as we need to for that. Email sent to a University of Kent address is also handled under the University’s own data protection arrangements.

Photographs and names

We show a person’s name or photograph on this website only with their agreement. If you appear here and would like your entry changed or removed, email us and we will do so.

Taking part in research

This website does not recruit research participants or collect research data. Anyone taking part in one of our studies receives that study’s own information sheet, which explains how their data is used.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other services, such as Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Figshare and publishers’ pages. They have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

Your rights and complaints

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used. Email us to do any of these. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
