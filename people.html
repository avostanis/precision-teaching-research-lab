Founder and lab lead

Dr Thanos Vostanis

Dr Thanos Vostanis is a Senior Lecturer at the Tizard Centre, University of Kent, specialising in intellectual and developmental disabilities. He is the founder of the PT Research Lab.

His work focuses on bringing precision teaching to professionals in many different contexts, and on evaluating its impact in both traditional and new areas of application, within and beyond education.

He has experience in both mainstream and special education. He is a qualified teacher (QTS), a certified behaviour analyst in the United Kingdom, UKBA(cert), a Board Certified Behaviour Analyst–Doctoral (BCBA-D) and a certified International Behavior Analyst (IBA). He has also undertaken advanced training in the CABAS® model and the Morningside Model of Generative Instruction.

Doctoral researchers: celerating

Not every doctoral project is about precision teaching. What they share is a focus on improving outcomes in the skills that help people live a good life. Projects cover evidence-based approaches to teaching, support for adults, staff performance and more, and we bring precision teaching into the work wherever it fits. In precision teaching terms they are celerating: their learning pictures are still climbing towards the aim.
