Work with us

Join or collaborate

There are two main ways to work with the lab. If you are interested in doctoral research or collaboration, please get in touch by email.

Doctoral supervision

Dr Vostanis welcomes enquiries from prospective doctoral researchers interested in precision teaching, fluency and agility, and evidence-based approaches to teaching and support. Please include a short outline of your proposed research.

Collaboration

We work with researchers, practitioners and services on shared studies, evaluations and training. Get in touch to discuss a project or a funding application.
