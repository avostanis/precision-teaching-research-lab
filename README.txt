PT Research Lab website
=======================

This folder is the whole website. Upload it as it is to any static web host.
How to edit, test and publish it is explained in the Website Maintenance Guide.

Pages you edit:        index.html (About), research.html, publications.html, people.html, impact.html, work-with-us.html
Shared parts you edit: SiteHeader.dc.html (menu), SiteFooter.dc.html (footer), ChartExplorer.dc.html (chart), JoinVisual.dc.html
Images:                assets/
Leave alone:           support.js, image-slot.js, vendor/, fonts/, .nojekyll, .image-slots.state.json

To look at the site on your own computer, see chapter 2 of the guide: the pages must be opened through a
small local web server, not by double-clicking them.
